How to progress
  • Add a rep before you add weight. Once every set hits the top of its rep range, add load next session and drop back to the bottom of the range.
  • On jump and sprint work, chase quality, not fatigue — stop a set early if height or speed drops off.
  • Deload every 5–6 weeks: same exercises, about 40% less volume, to let joints and the nervous system catch up.
Eating for +4kg
  • Run a modest surplus — roughly 250–350 kcal/day above maintenance. Bigger than that just adds fat, not the athletic build you're after.
  • Protein around 1.6–2.0g per kg bodyweight daily — about 90–110g now, drifting up as your weight climbs.
  • Weigh in weekly, same conditions. Trending faster than ~0.3kg/week — pull the surplus back. Slower than ~0.15kg/week — add ~150–200 kcal.

Bessent details "Operation Economic Outcast" sanctions campaign against Iran — oil prices fall on the news
Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent unveiled a wide-ranging package of secondary sanctions Monday targeting countries doing business with Iran, part of a campaign officials are calling "Operation Economic Outcast," warning at least one major financial institution could be sanctioned this week and that China would not be exempt. Oil actually fell on the announcement — WTI crude dropped about 2.5% to $84.89 a barrel and Brent lost a similar amount to $92.06 — as markets read the detailed rollout as lowering near-term escalation risk rather than raising it, with crude still flowing through the Strait of Hormuz.
CNBC ↗
U.S.-Canada trade talks stay collapsed as Carney vows retaliation
Trade talks between the U.S. and Canada remain broken off after collapsing over the weekend, with President Trump vowing to raise tariffs on Canadian-made autos, auto parts and steel to 50% starting Jan. 1, 2027. Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney has suspended negotiations and pledged like-for-like retaliatory tariffs on U.S. goods, escalating the cross-border trade dispute heading into the fall.
